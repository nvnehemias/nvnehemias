### 🚀 About Me

I'm a Data Analyst with experience bridging analytics engineering and business intelligence to transform raw data into reliable, actionable insights.  
My work focuses on building scalable data solutions — from designing data models and transformation workflows using SQL, Python, Snowflake, and dbt, to developing dashboards, reporting solutions, and analyses that help teams make data-driven decisions.  
This GitHub showcases projects across:  
- 🏗️ Analytics engineering: data modeling, transformations, testing, and scalable workflows  
- 📊 Data analytics: exploratory analysis, KPI development, and business insights  
- 🐍 Python: automation, data processing, and analytical workflows  
- 🗄️ SQL: complex analysis, querying, and data preparation  
- 📈 Business intelligence: dashboards and visualization solutions  
I enjoy working at the intersection of data engineering and analytics — building trustworthy data foundations while translating information into insights that help businesses grow.
